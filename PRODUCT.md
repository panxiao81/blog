# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Primary audience is split between readers and peers who are evaluating the author through the work. They come for multilingual essays, notes, and context about the person behind them, and they should feel that each locale is a first-class reading surface rather than a retrofit.

## Product Purpose
This site exists to publish authored writing in a plain multilingual format that builds trust in the author's thinking and craft. Success means readers can stay immersed in the text, move between locales confidently, and come away with a clear sense of both the ideas and the person writing them.

## Brand Personality
Technical, crisp, and self-possessed. The voice should feel exact, calm, and literate rather than ornamental, with enough personality to distinguish the author without competing with the writing.

## Anti-references
Do not let the site feel like a SaaS template or startup marketing surface. Avoid dashboard energy, generic product polish, conversion-driven framing, and interface patterns that make the writing feel secondary.

## Design Principles
- Let reading carry the identity.
- Make multilingual support feel native, not appended.
- Signal technical rigor through precision, not chrome.
- Keep the interface low-distraction and author-forward.
- Let profile context deepen the writing, not overshadow it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Locale-aware typography is a hard requirement, especially across Chinese, Japanese, and English reading contexts. The interface should stay low-distraction, readable for long-form sessions, and predictable in navigation, hierarchy, and copy so the site remains comfortable across languages and reading habits.
