This is a personal blog. I try to collect as little about you as possible, and the
parts that could identify you (analytics and advertising) stay off until you agree
to them. This page explains what is collected, by whom, and how to change your mind.

## Cookies and consent

When you first visit, analytics and advertising storage are **denied by default**
(via Google Consent Mode). Nothing is loaded into them until you press **Accept** on
the consent banner. If you press **Decline**, the site keeps working and those
services stay off.

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so you are not asked again on
every page. You can change it at any time with the **Privacy Settings** button in the
footer and at the bottom of this page.

## What is collected

- **Analytics — Google Analytics.** Only after you accept. It records anonymous usage
  such as the pages you view, rough location, and device/browser type, so I can see
  what people read. It is not used to identify you personally.
- **Advertising — Google AdSense.** Only after you accept. Google may set cookies to
  show and measure ads, including personalized ads where allowed. Ads appear only on
  list pages and at the end of posts.
- **Comments — Utterances / GitHub.** Comments are powered by Utterances and stored as
  issues in a public GitHub repository. Posting a comment requires a GitHub account
  and authorizing the Utterances app; your comment and GitHub username become public.
  This is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement.
- **Preferences.** Your theme choice and consent choice are kept in your browser's
  local storage only. They are never sent to a server.
- **Hosting logs.** The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes standard
  request data (such as IP address) to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

## Third-party services

These services have their own privacy policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
  [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)
- [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

You can manage Google ad personalization at
[Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Your choices

- Decline analytics and advertising at any time via **Privacy Settings**.
- Clear your browser's cookies and local storage to reset every choice.
- Avoid leaving a comment if you would rather not create a public GitHub record.
